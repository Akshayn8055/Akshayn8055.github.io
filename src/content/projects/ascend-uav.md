## Overview

Led Team WINGMEN (Alliance University, Bengaluru) to design and build a fully autonomous UAV for ISRO's Robotics Challenge — URSC 2026 (theme: **ASCEND**), selected as one of only **20 finalists from ~590 registered teams nationwide**. Custom-fabricated a lightweight carbon fibre airframe optimised for payload and aerodynamic efficiency.

## What it does

- **GPS-denied navigation** using an Intel RealSense D455 and Visual-Inertial Odometry (VIO), enabling waypoint-accurate flight, real-time object and feature detection, and precision autonomous landing on a designated pad.
- **Full autonomy stack** architected on an NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano interfaced with ArduPilot (ArduCopter) via MAVROS/ROS, achieving end-to-end mission execution: GPS-denied waypoint navigation, feature detection, autonomous landing, telemetry transfer to a base station, and wireless charging — closely simulating future planetary exploration missions.
- Demonstrated during the **Final Field Round (21–23 July 2026, URSC, Bengaluru)**, evaluated by a panel of senior ISRO scientists and engineers at the U R Rao Satellite Centre.

## Result

Top 20 National Finalist — ISRO Robotics Challenge (IRoC-U 2026), out of ~590 registered teams nationwide.
