## Overview

Built and programmed a computer-vision-guided pick-and-place cell on an ABB 6-axis industrial robotic arm, detecting object pose from a camera feed and driving the arm through calibrated pick and place motions.

## What it does

- Object detection and pose estimation feed real-time coordinates to the ABB controller.
- Applies robot operation and programming fundamentals from ABB's certified training to build a repeatable industrial automation cell.

Backed by a **Certification in ABB Robot Operation and Programming Training**.
